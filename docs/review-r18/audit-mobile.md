## 1. Summary

The mobile foundations are sound: per-finger input, safe areas, lifecycle pauses, a PWA and quality presets. But no part of the game has run on a phone, and it shows in three places:

1. **The touch path has correctness bugs that break play.**
   - Flight guns aim at whichever finger moved last, so touch-only players probably cannot clear `c5_m1` (M-01).
   - Phones boot on the keyboard scheme, so the first tips are shown in desktop wording (M-03).
   - Selecting the launcher silently stops auto-fire (M-08).
2. **Landscape phones were never laid out.**
   - The station's tab rail runs off the frame, so Star Map, Settings and Quit are unreachable on common phones, and Android locks the device to landscape (M-02).
   - The dialogue box, the HUD and the pause menu collide with each other and with the thumbs (M-09, M-10, M-15).
   - The rotate prompt neither blocks play nor stays on top (M-04).
3. **Automatic quality selection is wrong.**
   - The benchmark measures frame pacing, so every 60 Hz screen, and so every iPhone, gets `low`: DPR 1, 30 fps, no post-processing (M-06).
   - Frame skipping counts ticks rather than time: 15 fps in Low Power Mode, 120 fps of duplicate frames on 120 Hz phones, and full-rate rendering while paused (M-07).

## 3. Top 10 recommendations, ranked

1. **Stop touch pointers writing the aim** (M-01). One condition fixes flight shooting and `c5_m1` on phones.
2. **Lay out the station's tab rail for short landscape screens** (M-02), so Star Map, Settings and Quit are reachable at 293–430 px tall.
3. **Boot on the touch scheme and adopt the first touch** (M-03), and record tips per scheme.
4. **Fix auto quality and frame pacing** (M-06, M-07). Time GPU work instead of vsync. Pace frames by the wall clock, skip frames that ran no step, and go idle while paused.
5. **Make the rotate prompt a real block** (M-04): hold the world, cover the full screen, and sit above the pause menu.
6. **Add a history back-guard** (M-05), and pause on fullscreen exit and on blur (M-17).
7. **Adopt a thumb-arc touch HUD** (M-10), backed by a pairwise no-overlap E2E at six phone and tablet sizes.
8. **Fix pass-through dialogue and the pause layout on short screens** (M-09, M-15): an advance button, top-docked dialogue, and a Resume that is always reachable.
9. **Make the launcher a one-tap ability on touch and correct the touch wording** (M-08, M-13).
10. **Enlarge the quick-bar counts to 14 px and hold a wake lock during films** (M-11, M-12).

Before building, spend one half-hour on real devices — an iPhone and a 360-dp Android — to confirm M-01, M-02, M-06 and M-12, which are code-verified but never seen on hardware.

## 4. What is fine and should be protected

- **Input model.**
  - Per-pointer ownership, with capture and the zone decided at `pointerdown` (`TouchControls.ts:214-247`).
  - The floating stick with drift (`:296-311`).
  - Edges queue up so sub-frame taps and zero-step frames are never lost (`Input.ts:296-332`).
  - The 12 px tap slop (`Input.ts:81`).
- **Touch defaults.**
  - Auto-fire on touch, and auto-swap to the sidearm when the primary locks (`Settings.ts:167-168`, `Combat.ts:904-911`).
  - A heal at full HP is refused (E40).
  - Explosives on touch aim at the nearest enemy in clear line (`Surface.ts:2419-2474`).
- **Holds.** The full map, the quick picker and modal lines hold the simulation. This is the right model for phones; extend it (M-04, M-32) rather than remove it.
- **Viewport contract.**
  - `viewport-fit=cover`, a `100dvh` canvas, `touch-action: none` and `overscroll-behavior: none` (`index.html:5`, `style.css:5-53`).
  - Per-edge `max(Npx, env(...))` insets, locked by `tests/ui/viewport.test.ts`.
  - Menus reflow for portrait.
- **Lifecycle.**
  - Hiding the page pauses the loop, suspends audio, releases input and saves synchronously; only a tap resumes (`Game.ts:696-742`).
  - An `interrupted` iOS audio context retries on the next gesture (`Audio.ts:452-483`).
  - The context-loss overlay offers a reload after 5 s.
- **Renderer discipline.**
  - The context is opaque and not antialiased (`Renderer.ts:100-123`), with DPR caps.
  - Textures are clamped even when a scene loads them itself (`Flight.ts:500-510`).
  - The minimap repaints at 4 Hz (`Surface.ts:232`).
  - Particle caps are 60/150/300.
- **Loading.** The boot manifest is five files; planet art loads lazily; films are fetched whole into a Blob, so the service worker never answers a Range request. The precache (21 MB) is inside its 25 MB budget.
- **Accessibility.** Reduce-motion and reduced-transparency paths; `quality-low` drops the blur; 56 px touch buttons and tabs; confirm sheets on every purchase.
- **Tips.** Tips have both wordings, and the Controls sheet follows the scheme (`hints.ts`, `PauseMenu.ts:56-85`). The mechanism is right; M-03 and M-13 fix what it is fed.

## 5. Open questions for the designer

1. **Portrait gameplay.**
   - Keep phones landscape-only for play? I recommend yes, with M-04 making the block real.
   - The alternative is a designed portrait mode: a Hor+ camera, one-thumb play and the bar above the thumb.
2. **The touch HUD.** Approve the thumb-arc layout (M-10)?
   - It moves the quick bar off bottom-centre on touch, which needs an R-entry against PLAN §9 and R10.
   - Drop SWAP and ITEM on touch, since the bar already does both?
3. **The launcher on touch.** A one-tap ability (recommended, M-08), or keep select-then-fire?
4. **iOS silent switch.** Should the game play sound with the ringer off?
   - `navigator.audioSession.type = 'playback'` would, but it also stops the player's own music.
   - Today Web Audio follows the switch, so many iPhone players hear nothing (*unverified*).
5. **Resume on the planet after an OS kill** (M-24). This is an R-entry.
6. **First-visit download.** 21 MB of offline data on cellular without asking, or defer it until the station and show it in Settings (M-18)?
7. **Frame-rate policy.**
   - A user-facing "Battery saver (30 fps)" setting?
   - May adaptive quality lower the preset mid-session, and only ever downward (M-21)?
8. **Wake-lock scope.** Films (recommended, M-12)? Should the station also stay awake while the player reads the board or the shop?
9. **Haptics.** On by default for Android touch players (M-22)?
10. **Layout sizes to certify.** Which phone profiles are reference sizes for layout E2Es? I suggest Playwright's `iPhone 13 landscape` (750 × 342), `Pixel 5 landscape` (802 × 293) and `iPhone SE landscape` (568 × 320), plus 844 × 390 and 800 × 360. These should run as a `phone-landscape` project beside today's portrait-only `mobile` project (`playwright.config.ts:32,70-79`).
