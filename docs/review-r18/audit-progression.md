## 1. Summary

1. **Tokens stop mattering by chapter 4–5.** Bosses and elites hand out the whole rifle and armour ladder (500 tokens), and two of the five boss drops are guaranteed duplicates. The balance model "buys" those drops anyway. Only 845 of the 2,380-token sink is surface-relevant and not free, and a main-path player earns that by the end of chapter 5 (a completionist by the end of chapter 4).
2. **Build choice is shallow.**
   - The Marine's best build out-powers the other two classes by 23–43 % (DPS × EHP).
   - The Engineer's perks are discounts in a surplus economy, and its companion bonus reaches only the drone.
   - Agility and tech are worth 0.4–0.9 % per point, against 3–4 % for might and vigor.
   - One purchase, the Rotary Cannon, beats the whole rifle line.
3. **The most repeated objective is dead time.** The seven surface survive stages (585 s) run no waves, although PLAN §6 and §12 say they do. A 30-token Field Medic, or any cave, cancels every storm.
4. **The fix is one R18/SPEC-036 pass.**
   - Bosses drop one signature arsenal piece (first kill only), elites drop lithium and explosives, and the ladder is bought.
   - A "decision sink" invariant makes the regression testable.
   - Classes and attributes are retuned.
   - The arsenal is retuned behind a fire-rate fix.
   - Survive stages become holds against planet waves.
   - The Medic pauses in damaging weather.
5. **What moves.** Nothing changes the 670 / 104 / 2,380 token totals, the SPEC-010 invariants or the SPEC-016 worst-case pins. Only the optional kill-XP scaling (E-16) moves the completionist pin, from 11,135 XP at L19 to 11,756 XP at L20.
6. **Cheap extras.**
   - Items as rewards for side missions and for bonus objectives.
   - Replay "contracts" and a hard difficulty, for an endgame that is empty today.
   - Progression screens that tell the truth: sustained DPS, slot compare, the level's damage, refit advice.

## 5. What to protect

- **The anti-softlock spine.** The worst-case model:
  - loadout ≤ the worst case in every chapter, with margins 45 / 45 / 60 / 45 / 110;
  - the Ferrum gate at 39 % of its budget;
  - cargo ≥ the largest collect + 100;
  - start oil ≥ the Cinder-4 jump + 20;
  - vouchers that pay every jump;
  - E1's subsidy;
  - SPEC-034's shipping home for collects.

  None of this list is touched. Keep income (670 / 104 / 25 per level) and move the sinks instead.
- **Replays at 50 %** as E2's always-open safety net. Contracts sit on top and never replace them.
- **Lithium as the late sink:** tier-3 costs, the `c4_m3` delivery, demo charges and Ferrum's nodes. E-01 widens its sources without making it trivial.
- **Explosive crafting:** oil into frags and mines, lithium into charges. It is the best oil sink and a satisfying verb.
- **Boss XP of 100 + 100 × chapter,** and bosses as chapter climaxes. The set pieces are the variety highlights: the `c3_m2` escort, and the `c6_m2` defence and verdict.
- **The companion shape:** five companions, three levels each, one domain each; ARIA free; the Scanner Drone as the cheap QoL buy.
- **The shop's honesty machinery:** shortfall text, confirm sheets that re-validate, SPEC-035's human-worded deltas, and level-ups as toasts, never modals (E20).
- **R16's landing rule, and SPEC-032's skip-the-run.** E-10 labels the ship tiers rather than taking the skip away.
