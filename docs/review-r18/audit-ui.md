# ReaLLM — UI/UX and accessibility audit

> **Provenance.** Written on 2026-09-27 for PLAN R18, against a snapshot of `main` with SPEC-035 applied (the factory lane at `dc9310d`), which the report calls `scratchpad/game-r18`. Paths under `scratchpad/` were that review session's working folder. The decisions taken from this report are PLAN R18 and SPEC-036…SPEC-045 in `mdzunic/reallm-specs`.

**Date:** 2026-09-27 · **Build read:** `main` plus SPEC-035 in progress (snapshot `scratchpad/game-r18`), with SPEC-005, 014, 020, 026, 027, 028, 031, 034 and 035 · **Auditor:** Claude, as UI/UX and accessibility reviewer

**Method.**
- A read-only review of `src/ui/*`, `src/style.css`, `src/scenes/*`, `src/systems/UiHelpers.ts`, `src/core/Input.ts`, `src/core/Settings.ts`, `src/data/hints.ts`, `e2e/` and PLAN.
- Contrast ratios come from the WCAG formula, applied to the CSS tokens and to each planet's `palette.ground`. The ground colour stands in for the rendered ground.
- Colour-vision deficiency was simulated with the Machado 2009 matrices, and colour distance measured as ΔE76.
- No browser was run, and nothing in the repo was changed.

**Scope.** Content, flow, feedback, consistency, accessibility, desktop keyboard and mouse, gamepad, and how the UI adapts to width. Phone touch controls and mobile-browser issues belong to the other auditor. Items already done or in progress in SPEC-034 and SPEC-035 are not re-reported.

**Severity:**
- **P1:** most players hit it, or it blocks or endangers a group of players (keyboard-only, photosensitive, low vision).
- **P2:** noticeable friction or confusion.
- **P3:** polish.

**Effort:** S ≤ ½ day · M 1–3 days · L 1–2 weeks.

---

## 1. Summary

- **The base is strong:** one token theme, focus rings throughout, glyph-backed colour pairs, near-total reduce-motion coverage, captions always on, re-validating confirm sheets, and refusal lines that name the shortfall.
- **Play hides its feedback.** A finished mission is a chime and nothing on screen. Loot and active buffs are silent. The quick bar's heat, charges, counts and key hints are 8 px. HUD text and the waypoint drop to 1.05–2.6 : 1 on Cinder-4, Vetra and Eden.
- **Safety.** Storm ticks re-fire the full-edge red damage flash at 2–4 Hz, which breaks WCAG 2.3.1. No setting stops it.
- **Keyboard and pad play is not yet first-class.** Modal dialogue advances only on a click and shows no cue. Every re-render drops focus: five attribute points take about 140 Tabs. Escape leaks through sheets. There is no gamepad driver.
- **A hint points at a missing setting.** "Casual difficulty is in Settings" is shown to dying players, and the setting does not exist (PLAN §4 says it should).
- **Settings coverage.** Some settings are stored but unreachable, and the standard accessibility set is missing. Reduce motion is one switch doing five jobs.
- **Findings:** 43 in all — 7 P1, 23 P2 and 13 P3. The top ten are all S effort, about 4–5 days together.

## 4. What to protect

- **The token theme.** One `:root` block, and "a retheme is a token swap" is pinned by `tests/ui/theme.test.ts`. It is also what makes U-40's colour-blind preset cheap. Keep the console frame of SPEC-031 exactly as is; the proposals here only add tokens and opt-in classes.
- **The HUD architecture.** Systems write a plain `HudModel`, and `flush()` diffs it after render. Bars move by `transform`, and numbers are written only on change. Every HUD proposal above (effects, target, low HP) goes through the model, never through DOM reads in `update()`.
- **Focus and keys already done right:**
  - `#ui :focus-visible` rings on every control, plus the key underline.
  - Physical key codes (AZERTY-safe), with text fields exempt from bindings.
  - Throttle-down off Ctrl.
  - Arrow keys walk the menu and the star map.
  - The map, picker and gear card capture Escape correctly; they are the model for U-20.
- **Colour-blind pairs backed by glyphs:** `♥` / `⛨` / `▲`, `✓` / `✗` requirements, resource initials on map nodes, and the wallet's at-cap signalled three ways (weight, hue, `▲`).
- **Reduce-motion coverage is near complete.** Every CSS animation has a static form (toast, sheet, lock pulse, hit wedge, low-HP, cargo, tracker, waypoint, static burst, spinner, respawn, autopilot, card glitch, reveal bars, screen fade, films, dialogue glitch). JS honours it for the typewriter, shake, bob, kick, launch pose, star streaks, clouds, lightning and grain. U-37 only splits it; it removes nothing.
- **Film captions.** Always on, DOM-rendered, speaker name plus colour, a live region for screen readers, and posters → text fallbacks.
- **Refusals that say exactly what is missing:**
  - `Need 40 more tokens` and `Need 20 more oil`.
  - The depart reason, and the pad's R16 empty text.
  - `HP full`, `Field use only`, `Mine limit reached`.
  - The balance line in every purchase sheet (`Tokens 340 → 291`).
  - Re-validation on confirm (14-c).
- **Empty states are handled:** `No active missions`, `Empty — buy a launcher in the shop`, `Pick a class to see the numbers.`, the terminal's reason line, and `No missions here right now.`
- **Guidance and holds.** Tips worded per scheme; the guidance level setting; the stuck ladder; the map, picker and modal lines holding the simulation; the SPEC-034 recall with its sheet.
- **Toast coalescing** (`×N`) and the `role="status"` rack.
- **44 / 56 px targets and the 14 px body floor.** U-10 and U-38 raise the HUD's exemptions; they never lower the floor.

## Appendix C — Where each proposal touches the hot path

None adds a DOM read to `update()`:
- **U-03, U-04, U-14** write `HudModel` fields that `flush()` diffs after render.
- **U-09** keeps a timestamp in `Hud.damageFlash()`.
- **U-10, U-11, U-12, U-13, U-15, U-38** are CSS and root custom properties, set on settings change.
- **U-24** reads `navigator.getGamepads()` once per frame into existing state, with no allocation. It is not a DOM read.
- **U-19, U-20, U-21** run in UI event handlers only.
