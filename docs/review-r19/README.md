# The R19 review (2026-09-27)

The third design review of ReaLLM, run on `main` at `2cf398a` (SPEC-035 and PLAN R18 merged). It looked at the story as a player meets it, the films between chapters, the look of the world, three new mechanics (running, the underground, puzzles), treasure, New Game+ and what brings a player back. Its findings became PLAN R19 (the story), R20 (the world) and R21 (the next instance), and the specs SPEC-046…SPEC-059 (milestones M7h, M7i and M7j).
